# Keyword Integration and ATS Workflow

## Mandatory rules
1. Every JD keyword gets woven into the resume before delivery — either integrated into bullet text/Core Competencies, or explicitly confirmed by Melissa as a gap that can't be claimed.
2. Every primary JD outcome maps to at least one named bullet. A keyword sitting only in Core Competencies doesn't satisfy this.

## Workflow — runs on every JD paste before first delivery
1. Extract every JD phrase and critical concept noun from the full JD.
2. Categorize each: JD-exact / critical concept / tool-or-system / honest gap.
3. Grep the assembled resume in three passes:
   - Pass 1: exact JD phrase, case-insensitive.
   - Pass 2: root/variant check ("center of excellence" should match "centers of excellence").
   - Pass 3 (HRBP/Director targets only): confirm these appear in bullet text, not just Core Competencies — corrective action, internal investigations, employment law, conflict resolution, client groups, people managers, inclusive culture, Centers of Excellence, change management, change communication plans, accommodations, incentive plan design (when the JD names it).
4. Integrate any missing keyword naturally into bullet text or Core Competencies wherever the source work actually supports it.
5. If a keyword implies a tool not in the source resume, a specific industry, or a deliverable with unclear scope — stop and ask Melissa before writing anything.
6. Re-run the metric cap check after every keyword patch.
7. Mark something an honest gap only when: the tool is on the banned list, the experience is genuinely absent, or Melissa confirms she can't credibly claim it.

**When to ask Melissa:** "The JD requires [keyword]. Does your source work at [role] support this? Specifically: [what the claim would say]."

## Keyword change log — deliver with every rewrite, four columns
Added (exact placement — role and bullet number) | Already Covered (frequency + location) | Honest Gaps (reason: tool ban / experience gap / Melissa confirmed) | Asked and Confirmed (what was asked, what Melissa confirmed).

## ATS Scanner + Live-Market Keyword Workflow

Fires when Melissa asks for an ATS score/scan, "how will this parse," a live-market keyword pull, or a rescore-and-grill after a rewrite. Standalone four-step add-on — can run before the tailoring workflow (to find gaps) or after (to confirm the lift). Doesn't replace the Council or Humanize passes; those still apply to any bullets this workflow produces.

**Step 1 — ATS parser score.** Act as an ATS scanner (Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo). Score out of 100 the way the software actually parses: keyword match to the target role, readable formatting, clear section headers, a clean contact block, no tables/columns the parser struggles with. Output the score, then the five gaps costing the most points, ranked, with one fix line each. Be strict.

**Step 2 — Live-market keyword pull.** Act as a recruiter hiring for the target role in the target location. Search live job listings from this week, pull the skills/tools/phrases that repeat across them. Output the twenty most-repeated keywords ranked, flag which are missing from the resume, and give the exact market phrasing to add.

**Step 3 — Bullet rewrite with real numbers.** Rewrite every bullet using the missing keywords from Step 2. Strong verb opener, a real number in every bullet (percent, dollars, headcount, time saved, volume). If a number hasn't been given, ask before writing — never invent one. Show old vs. new, one role at a time. Route every rewrite through the XYZ formula, verb rules, and banned-word list in `bullet-formula-and-verbs.md` and `facts-and-banned-words.md`, and through Council Condition B and the humanize pass, same as any other rewrite.

**Step 4 — Rescore and mock interview.** Rerun Step 1 against the finished resume using the identical method, to confirm the score actually moved. Then predict the ten most likely interview questions for the target role and run a mock interview, one question at a time, scored out of ten, one fix, then the next question.

**Minimum passing score: 92/100.** Below that, the resume isn't delivery-ready — loop back to Step 3, rewrite the lowest-scoring gaps, rerun Step 1, repeat until 92+. Never present a sub-92 resume as final.

**Hard rules:** never invent a number, always ask and wait for the real one. Fact-check every certification claim against what Melissa actually holds before it goes in a rewrite or a mock-interview answer. All other rules in this skill (banned words, metric caps, XYZ structure, level framing, Council/Humanize gates) still apply to everything drafted here.
