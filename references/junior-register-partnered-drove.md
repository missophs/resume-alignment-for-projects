# Junior Register — Partnered and Drove

**Who this is for:** Sr. People Partner/HRBP and Director HRBP targets — a CHRO, CPO, VP of People, or any other People leader sits above Melissa in the org chart, whatever that person's exact title. She is not the top People leader.

**Role summary openers:**
- Sr. People Partner/HRBP: "Partnered with," "Drove"
- Director HRBP: "Drove," "Partnered with"

"Owned" and "Built" are both off the table at this register — not a softer version of them, a different pair of openers entirely.

## No ownership-implying verb+object pairing, regardless of which verb
Don't pair any verb — including "Drove," approved above — with a high-ownership object: a team, a function's strategy, an M&A transaction, a budget, an org-wide process someone introduced, or anything else implying Melissa held final authority over it. This overrides even a verb generally approved at this register when the specific object makes the bullet read as outright ownership. The pattern is the rule, not a fixed word list: if a bullet reads like Melissa owned the outcome rather than contributed to it, rewrite with "Partnered with" and reframe the object to reflect a contributing role.

**Exception:** only claim direct ownership language if the target JD explicitly and specifically requires that exact claim (e.g., a JD stating "managed a team") — and even then, apply the exception narrowly to what the JD actually asked for, not to the rest of the bullet.

**Known examples of this pattern, already caught and corrected:**
- "Drove [People/HR] strategy" → use "Partnered with" for strategy claims.
- "Steered a [team/department]" → banned by default; only permitted if the JD specifically says "managed a team."
- "Led [M&A transactions]" → "Led" is a senior-register verb (see `senior-register-bullets-dash-verbs-lead.md`); use "Partnered with deal teams on..." instead.
- "Realigned [a budget]" or "Replaced [a system/framework]" — describes restructuring an entire budget or framework outright (see WPP's "Realigned a $9.5M HR budget" and "Replaced inconsistent goal-setting with one performance calibration framework" in `role-wpp.md` — correct as written at the senior register, needs reframing here). Use "Partnered with" and describe contributing to the change, not owning it.
- "Incorporated/brought in workforce planning" as something Melissa introduced org-wide (see DHW's "Incorporated workforce planning into quarterly business reviews" in `role-dhw.md` — same situation, correct at the senior register) — a junior, reporting-in person wouldn't have introduced a new planning function. Reframe as partnering on an existing or emerging workforce-planning effort, not introducing it.

A new case that fits this same pattern gets the same fix — reframe with "Partnered with" — rather than a new one-off ban added to this list.

**Bullets:** the same XYZ dash formula as the senior register — action and metric, semicolon, named mechanism — with a strong action verb leading every bullet, from the same verb bank in `bullet-formula-and-verbs.md`. Bullet construction doesn't change between registers; only the role summary opener and job title change (see the level-conditional titles in `role-cprime.md` and `role-wpp.md`).
