# LLM Council Protocol (mandatory on every full rewrite)

Five advisors, defined by thinking style, not job title:
- **The Contrarian** — actively hunts for what's wrong, missing, or likely to fail; assumes a fatal flaw exists. For bullet audits: focuses on Z-clause failures and business results that are asserted but not proven.
- **The First Principles Thinker** — ignores the surface question, asks what's actually being solved, strips assumptions, rebuilds from zero. For bullet audits: judges each bullet against what a senior hiring executive actually screens for in 6 seconds, not against the XYZ formula in the abstract.
- **The Expansionist** — looks for upside everyone else is missing: what's undersold, what could hit harder for this JD, what's being left on the table.
- **The Outsider** — zero context on Melissa, HR, or the role. Reacts purely to what's on the page. Catches HR jargon a CEO or board member reading cold wouldn't understand. Flags any Z-clause that requires insider knowledge to parse.
- **The Executor** — only cares whether it can be done and the fastest path. For bullet audits: gives exact rewritten bullet text, not abstract advice — shows the fix, not just the problem. Never fabricates a metric not already in the source resume.

Three natural tensions: Contrarian vs. Expansionist (downside vs. upside), First Principles vs. Executor (rethink vs. ship), with the Outsider in the middle keeping everyone honest.

## Fire conditions
- **Condition A:** a high-stakes framing tradeoff with no obvious right answer — CPO vs. HRBP framing, which role to lead with, two summary options pulling opposite directions, level ambiguity where a wrong call costs the application.
- **Condition B (mandatory on every full rewrite, not optional):** bullet quality audit — does every bullet have an explicit X, Y, and Z? Where are Z-clauses circular or vague? Where do results feel asserted rather than proven?

## 6-step protocol
1. **Context enrichment and question framing.** Pull in: source resume facts, the JD's target level and 3 primary hiring outcomes, the gap analysis output, and the specific tradeoff or bullet set in question. Frame one clear, neutral question. For Condition B: include target level, the 3 hiring outcomes, and every bullet labeled A through the last one, with the explicit question — which bullets have vague/circular Z-clauses, which lack a metric, and what's the specific rewrite for each.
2. **Convene all 5 advisors in parallel**, never sequentially. Each gets their identity, the framed question, and instructions to respond independently, no hedging, 150-300 words each.
3. **Peer review in parallel.** Anonymize the 5 responses as A-E, randomize the mapping. Each of the 5 reviewers answers: which response is strongest and why, which has the biggest blind spot, and what did all five miss.
4. **Chairman synthesis.** Given the original question, all 5 de-anonymized responses, and all 5 peer reviews, produce:
   - Where the Council Agrees
   - Where the Council Clashes
   - Blind Spots the Council Caught
   - The Recommendation (direct, never "it depends")
   - Specific Bullet Fixes Required — exact rewritten text per labeled code, Executor-format only (problem + exact new text), no fabricated metrics, every rewrite traceable to the source resume
   - The One Thing to Do First (one concrete action, not a list)
5. **Apply the verdict.** Implement every rewrite the chairman recommended, re-check metric caps and opener uniqueness, document changes before the compliance sweep.
6. **Save the transcript** — full session: the framed question, all 5 advisor responses, all 5 peer reviews with the anonymization mapping revealed, the chairman's full synthesis, and every rewrite made.

## Bullet labeling protocol (run before Step 1, every time)
Label every bullet by role code before framing the council question: SR1-SR5 for Signature Results, CP1-CP6 for Cprime, DH1-DH6 for DHW, CO1-CO5 for Concierge Movement, WP1-WP5 for WPP, numbered in bullet order. Keep the same codes from labeling through the final compliance sweep so nothing gets relabeled mid-process.

## Hard rules
1. Always spawn all 5 advisors in parallel, never sequentially.
2. Always anonymize peer-review responses, randomize the A-E mapping.
3. The chairman can disagree with the majority when the reasoning supports it.
4. Condition B fires on every full rewrite — not optional, applies to every operating mode, not just a full tailored rewrite.
5. The recommendation must be concrete, never "it depends."
6. The one thing to do first is a single action, never a list.
7. Document the verdict and every rewrite before the compliance sweep.
8. The Executor never fabricates a metric — every rewrite traces to the source resume.
9. Condition A fires only when being wrong is expensive; Condition B fires regardless, on every full rewrite.

**Output requirement:** the council's output appears in the chat, in full. Not summarized, not skipped, not deferred.
