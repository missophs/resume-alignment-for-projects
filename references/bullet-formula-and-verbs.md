# XYZ Bullet Formula, Verb Rules, Signature Results, Anti-Redundancy

## XYZ formula
X (accomplishment, active verb, outcome headline) + Y (exact metric — %, $, or time) + Z (a specific named method/tool/action — must be explicit, not implied).

Semicolon Z-clause workaround for the "by [gerund]" ban: `[X accomplishment], [Y metric]; [Z method/tool named directly, no gerund]`.

The Z-clause is non-negotiable. Every bullet states how the outcome was achieved — the specific action, tool, process, or mechanism. A metric with no named method is a task description, not proof. Z can't restate X or Y in different words, can't be generic ("through strong leadership"), and can't be omitted. If no honest Z exists, rewrite the bullet until one does — never fabricate one.

**X, Y, and Z must each be fully flushed out — none of the three gets compressed into a fragment to save words.** X states the accomplishment with enough specificity to mean something on its own, not just a verb and a noun. Y gives the exact metric with the context it needs (not a bare number sitting alone). Z names the actual mechanism specifically enough that someone could picture the real process, not a vague gesture at one. A bullet where any of the three reads as two or three throwaway words is under-flushed and gets rewritten, regardless of which structure pattern it's using.

## Bullet structure rotation — the four roles must not all read the same shape
X, Y, and Z are always present, but the ORDER they appear in is now assigned per role, so Professional Experience doesn't read as one bullet architecture repeated four times. Three patterns rotate:

**Standard — action and metric, then mechanism after the semicolon:** X, Y; Z.
Example: "Reduced labor cost inflation 17% across three client organizations, mapping every seat against the finance plan; ten duplicate management layers removed once client executives could see who actually reported to whom."

**Metric-first — the number opens the bullet, mechanism and action follow:** Y; Z, X.
Example: "Labor cost inflation cut 17% across three client organizations; a Finance-partnered, seat-by-seat mapping exercise surfaced ten duplicate management layers before any headcount decision went final."

**Mechanism-first — the method opens as a noun phrase, action and metric follow:** Z; X, Y.
Example: "A Finance-partnered seat-mapping review across three client organizations, run before any headcount decision went final, removed ten duplicate management layers and cut labor cost inflation 17%."

Never open a mechanism-first bullet with a gerund ("By mapping...") — that trips the banned by-[gerund] grep. Open with a noun phrase instead ("A Finance-partnered seat-mapping review...", "A quarterly-review-embedded workforce plan...").

**Assignment — one pattern per role:**
- Cprime → Standard
- DHW → Metric-first
- Concierge Movement → Mechanism-first
- WPP → Standard (the deliberate repeat — three named patterns can't cover four roles without one repeat; WPP shares Cprime's pattern rather than DHW's or Concierge's because WPP's turnaround story and Cprime's transaction-readiness story share no content, so the shared skeleton doesn't make the two roles blur together)

Keep every bullet in a role on that role's assigned pattern — don't mix patterns within one role. Signature Results bullets are exempt: as cross-career aggregations, each one can use whichever pattern reads strongest on its own.

**Bullet limits:** under 280 characters, no period at the end, no line cap — a bullet can wrap more than one line. A fully flushed-out X, Y, Z bullet will typically run toward the longer end of that range — don't trim it back down to hit an artificially short target.

**Filler removal:** strip adjectives with no number attached, redundant context, hedging words, and connector-only transition words from every bullet.

**Interior banned words (anywhere in a sentence, not just as an opener):** supported, helped, contributed, partnered with. This bullet-text ban does not apply to role summary sentences — see the exception in `facts-and-banned-words.md`.

If Z restates X or Y in different words, or names a vague process with no concrete noun, mark it Fail and rewrite before the table closes. ## Verb rules
All bullet openers across the full resume (5 Signature Results bullets + 5-6 per role) must be unique — treat repeats as a delivery-blocking defect. The Summary's S1 verb can't repeat as any bullet opener. Never repeat an opening verb within the same role.

**Verb bank** (trimmed, halted, elevated, unified, extended, piloted, architected, transformed, expanded, and anchored are banned by Melissa — removed from every category below, do not add them back):
- Revenue/Growth: Scaled, Lifted, Grew, Launched, Built, Improved, Drove
- Cost/Efficiency/Risk: Cut, Reduced, Eliminated, Closed, Compressed, Lowered, Stripped, Recovered
- Stabilization/Turnaround: Reversed, Stabilized, Realigned, Restored, Repositioned, Reset
- Systems/Architecture: Engineered, Designed, Standardized (banned as an opener specifically, see `facts-and-banned-words.md` — usable mid-sentence only), Integrated, Codified, Operationalized, Analyzed
- Leadership/Culture: Coached, Resolved, Aligned, Developed, Mentored, Calibrated, Guided

**Acceptable summary openers:** Owned, Built, Led, Reversed, Closed, Delivered, Eliminated, Restructured, Scaled, Drove (sparingly). "Stabilized"/"Stabilizing" are banned as a role summary opener even though they appear in the verb bank above — the verb bank is for bullets, not summary openers. "Anchored" is banned outright, don't use it anywhere.

## Role summary rules
One sentence, impact statement not job description, 15-20 words target, 22-word absolute max. No overlap with the scope line above or bullet 1 below. Active verb + specific scope + specific outcome, no trailing participials, no gerund chains after a comma, at least one JD-aligned keyword the source work actually supports.

**Fractured-construction test:** if either half of an "X and Y" construction could be deleted and the sentence would still make sense on its own, it's fractured (both halves saying the same thing). Clean version: action + result, where the second half depends on the first.

## Signature Results rules
5 bullets, context-free, ordered by JD priority (not chronology, not dollar size), cross-career aggregations only — never restate a role bullet. Must differ from every role bullet in verb, framing, and angle. Any bullet with 55%+ word-set overlap with a role bullet gets rewritten. Same XYZ formula, same process-language ban, same semicolon Z-clause rule.

## Mandatory Signature Results redundancy table — run before every delivery
This is the actual mechanism SKILL.md Step 7 and the compliance checklist point to — a real check with a verdict per row, not a read-through. A checklist line saying "Signature Results aren't near-clones of role bullets" isn't a mechanism on its own; this table is. Build it once all 5 Signature Results and every role bullet are drafted:

| SR Code | Core claim | Closest-matching role bullet (code) | Word-set overlap % | Verdict |

For each of the 5 Signature Results bullets: find its single closest-matching role bullet across all four roles, estimate word-set overlap using substantive words only (metrics, mechanisms, nouns — not connector words), and mark Fail at 55%+ overlap. Also mark Fail if the underlying claim is the same fact restated with a different verb or a reordered metric — a paraphrase is still a duplicate even when the word-overlap percentage comes in under 55%. Any Fail gets rewritten with a genuinely different angle, not just different wording, then that row re-runs before the table closes. The table appears in chat in full, same as the XYZ verification table — it doesn't get summarized, skipped, or assumed clean.

## Anti-redundancy rules
**Within a role:** no bullet repeats an outcome already stated in another bullet or the role summary; no two bullets share a mechanism/method/tool angle.

**Across roles:** no achievement appears in two different roles. Each role keeps a distinct mechanism, business context, and outcome angle — see the per-role story angle noted in each `role-*.md` file.

**Cross-role redundancy gate — run before every delivery, not a mental check:**
1. Story audit: write one sentence stating each role's core claim, confirm no two share it even with different numbers.
2. Signature Results paraphrase check: confirm no Signature Results bullet restates a role bullet's framing in paraphrase — a reworded metric on the same underlying claim is still a duplicate.
3. Role summary echo check: confirm role summaries don't share clustered root words, even without an exact repeated phrase.
4. If two roles are converging on the same narrative, redistribute the underlying facts before final bullets are written, not after.

**Metrics cap:** every distinctive metric appears at most twice across the whole document (see the cap list in `facts-and-banned-words.md`). Any third occurrence is removed immediately, and the cap gets re-checked after every keyword patch.
